Origins · Lingagiri Connection

The Mantripragada family traces its roots to Lingagiri (Lingairi). The founding ancestor documented in family records is Mantripragada Narasimha Rao (born c. 1816, Dhatha Nama Samvatsara, Vaishakha Pournami), referred to as the Mula Purusha (founding father). A significant chapter involves the adoption of Venkatrama Rao (Ramanuja Rao) from Lingagiri into the family, which linked the Lingagiri line to the Mantripragada household. The family is also associated with the Kankipati and Aandallu communities through marital bonds.
