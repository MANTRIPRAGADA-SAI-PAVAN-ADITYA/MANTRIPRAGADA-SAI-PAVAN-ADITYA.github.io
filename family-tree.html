Family Origins

Lingagiri Connection

The Mantripragada family traces its roots to Lingagiri (Lingairi). The founding ancestor documented in family records is Mantripragada Narasimha Rao (born c. 1816, Dhatha Nama Samvatsara, Vaishakha Pournami), referred to as the Mula Purusha — the founding father. A pivotal chapter involves the adoption of Venkatrama Rao (birth name: Ramanuja Rao) from Lingagiri into the household of Narasimha Rao (1872), linking the Lingagiri line to the Mantripragada household. His son Narasimha Rao (1923) was later given in adoption to Venkaiah (the brother of Narasimha Rao 1872), creating two distinct lineage branches that continue to the present day.
